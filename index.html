About the Club

We're a group of ALU Rwanda students who got tired of just reading about AI and decided to actually build things with it. The Claude Builder Club is where that happens — we get together, pick apart how Claude works under the hood, and ship stuff that matters to us as students.

You don't need to be a CS major or know what an API is to show up. Some of us came in barely knowing how to write a prompt. What we have in common is that we'd rather learn by doing than sit through another lecture. If that sounds like you, pull up.

What is Claude?

Claude is an AI assistant built by Anthropic, designed to be helpful, harmless, and honest. It can assist with writing, analysis, coding, math, and creative tasks — all through natural conversation.

Natural Conversation

Chat with Claude like you would with a knowledgeable colleague. It understands context and nuance.

Code Generation

Write, debug, and explain code across dozens of programming languages.

Research & Analysis

Summarize documents, analyze data, and help you think through complex problems.

Safe by Design

Built with safety at its core. Anthropic leads AI safety research to ensure responsible AI.
